How the final reports behave

Each team gets a standalone report, one HTML file: points out of 70, the ending its decisions earned, the conduct ledger, the act-by-act table, a verification ID, and your remarks and signature block. It opens in any browser and prints to PDF in one click. The cohort zip bundles every report; the CSV carries the full item record, team by team, for the follow-on programme. Sending opens a prefilled email in your own mail client; attach the downloaded report before it goes.
